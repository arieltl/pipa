# AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. The person submitting a contribution remains responsible for its correctness, safety, licensing, and maintenance implications.

## Contributor responsibilities

- Understand and review every submitted change. Be able to explain its behavior and tradeoffs; do not submit unreviewed generated output.
- Test changes in proportion to their risk, following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). State what you tested and any checks you could not run. Generated tests do not replace reviewing the implementation.
- Disclose substantial AI assistance in the pull request: briefly identify the tool and which parts it helped produce, such as implementation, tests, documentation, or design. Minor spelling corrections and routine autocomplete do not need disclosure.
- Never send secrets, credentials, real invoices, customer information, or other private data to AI tools as part of project work. Use synthetic examples.
- Only contribute material you have the right to submit under the project's license. Check generated code and documentation for inappropriate copying, incompatible licensing, and unsupported claims.

Disclosure does not require publishing private conversations or full prompts. A short note is enough, for example: “Used an AI coding assistant to draft validation tests; I reviewed the cases and ran the test suite.”

## Review expectations

AI-assisted and manually written contributions are held to the same standards. Maintainers may request explanations, additional tests, or a smaller patch, and may decline work that cannot be adequately reviewed. AI assistance is not a substitute for human accountability.
